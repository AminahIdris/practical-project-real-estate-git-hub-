# Lead Management

## Purpose

Create, update, retrieve, qualify, assign, and track real-estate leads.

## Lead Lifecycle

``` text
NEW
 ↓
CONTACTED
 ↓
QUALIFIED
 ↓
FOLLOW_UP
 ↓
CONVERTED / LOST
```

The exact status model may be expanded only when business requirements
justify it.

## Required Capabilities

-   create lead
-   retrieve lead
-   update lead
-   list/search/filter leads
-   assign lead
-   view score
-   view conversation
-   create follow-up
-   record activity

## Data Rules

Do not overwrite the original customer message.

AI-derived requirements may be updated as more conversation information
becomes available.
